## Summary

ResizeMan processes videos and GIFs **only on your Mac**. We do **not** collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data.

---

## Data we collect

**We do not collect any data.**

The app does not require an account, sign-in, analytics, advertising, or internet connectivity to perform video or GIF conversion.

---

## Data on your device

- Videos and GIFs you select are read from paths you choose and written to output locations you choose.
- Processing runs locally with bundled FFmpeg on your Mac.
- We do not upload your files to our servers or any third-party service.

---

## Permissions

The app may request access to files and folders you explicitly select through standard macOS open/save dialogs. The app uses the App Sandbox and only accesses user-selected paths.

---

## Third parties

ResizeMan does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

FFmpeg is included in the app bundle for local media processing under its applicable open-source license.

---

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
